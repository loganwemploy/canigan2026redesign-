## Blocked claims

- Protein prevents facial, breast, hip, or buttock volume loss.
- A supplement treats medication side effects or replaces medical nutrition counseling.
- Medication users will preserve muscle by using protein without resistance exercise.
- Any named or unnamed prescription is safe in pregnancy.
- Weight loss should be pursued during pregnancy.
- Joining as an Ambassador means "getting paid to lose weight."
- Recruiting people itself produces compensation.
- Any income amount is typical, guaranteed, easy, passive, or available without meaningful sales work and expenses.
- Shaklee-funded or product-specific research is independent research.

## Audience evidence limitation

Confidence that five specific audiences are historically the "highest converting across all MLMs" is `0.78`, below the project threshold. No reliable cross-company conversion dataset was found. The build must not present that as fact. Audience priority is instead an explicit strategy inference from: wellness representing 31% of 2024 U.S. direct-selling sales, women representing 73% of participants, high-protein and healthy-aging demand, Canigan's product fit, and Candy's credible lived experience. See the [2026 Journal of Direct Selling Research](https://dsef.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/JDSR-Winter-2026-FULL.pdf) and [NielsenIQ's 2025 wellness report](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/news-center/2025/niq-report-reveals-2025-global-health-wellness-trends/).
